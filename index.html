Mini Pixel

A free, browser-based pixel art editor and sprite animation maker — made by Time And Time Studio.

Mini Pixel is a lightweight online pixel art tool for drawing sprites, icons, game assets, and animated pixel art directly in your browser. There's nothing to install and nothing to sign up for — open it and start drawing. Everything you make is saved locally in your own browser, so your projects are private and available offline.

What can you do here?

  • Draw & erase — Pencil, Eraser and Eyedropper tools with an adjustable 1–64px brush size, shared between pencil and eraser.
  • Full color control — a color wheel with a brightness slider, plus a hex input for exact colors.
  • Mirror / symmetry drawing — turn on Mirror X and/or Mirror Y to draw symmetrical sprites and patterns automatically.
  • Undo & redo — full history so you can experiment freely.
  • Resizable canvas — set any custom width and height for your sprite.
  • Multi-frame animation — add, copy, and delete frames, then preview your animation at any FPS and export every frame at once. Perfect for game sprite sheets and animated pixel art.
  • Import & export PNG — bring in an existing PNG to edit, or export your pixel art to a PNG file.
  • Pan, zoom & rotate — on touch, pinch with two fingers to zoom/rotate and drag to pan; with a mouse, scroll the wheel to zoom, hold the middle button and drag to rotate, or hold the right button and drag to pan. You can also pick a preset zoom level from 1× up to 64×. One tap/click resets the view.
  • Save multiple projects — name and save as many projects as you like; they're stored in your browser and reload instantly.
  • Works with touch, mouse, or keyboard — a bottom toolbar puts the main drawing tools within thumb's reach on phones and tablets.

Who is it for?

Mini Pixel is built for game developers, pixel artists, hobbyists, and anyone who wants a fast, free, no-fuss pixel art editor and sprite animation maker that runs entirely in the browser — no downloads, no accounts, no ads getting in the way of drawing.

Mini Pixel is developed and maintained by Time And Time Studio.
